1. Taming
Lv 1 Skill; No Limit
MP Cost: 300
Damage Type: None
Skill Effect:
- Allows you to capture a pet by using a Cage (you must have a Cage in your inventory)

- The base values for pet capturing are the following:
Timer = Skill Timer Boost + mob's Neutral Proration Rate/2 

Maximum Taming Gauge = 10000 + 10 * mob's Physical Proration Rate 

Mob Resistance Increase = (10 + mob's Magic Proration Rate/10)/3 

Mob Level Limit = Player Level - 10 

Taming Gauge starts at 0 

Skill Timer Boost starts at 25 seconds (levels 1 to 5) or 30 seconds (levels 6 to 10) 

Mob Resistance starts at 0; it cannot go under 0 and cannot go over 1100 

- If the mob starts moving, the Mob Resistance is continually increased by the Mob Resistance Increase until it stops moving; if it stops moving, the Mob Resistance is continually decreased by 9 until it starts moving again

- If the mob isn't damaged during the capture, the Mob Resistance will be continually decreased by 9 until it is damaged; if damaging the mob stops that from happening completely or simply sets a cooldown for this mechanic is unknown 

- The Taming Gauge continually increases or decreases according to the following formula:
Base Taming Gauge Increase = (1 - mob's Current HP/mob's Max HP) * 300
 
Taming Gauge Increase/Decrease = Base Taming Gauge Increase * (1000 - Mob Resistance)/1000 

- If the mob has the Tumble, Stun, Sleep, Stop or Fatigue aliment, the Taming Gauge Increase/Decrease is doubled

- If you succeed to raise the gauge to the maximum before the timer runs out, the pet will be captured; the pet will have a randomly attributed weapon type, nature and potential base; the type of the mob determines which attack patterns it has; to use the pet, you must first purchase your land, release your pet from the cage at your land, and then raise the pet before calling it

- If you fail, the mob will be inflicted with the Rage ailment and you have a chance to lose the Cage; leveling this skill decreases the chance of losing the Cage (full effects unknown)

- You cannot capture a mob that has the Rage ailment or that has a level higher than the Mob Level LimitPassive Effect: Mob Level Limit +(Skill Level)

Game Description: "Capture the monsters weaker than you. The Max Lv of them and the time limit increase. The chance of breaking Cages decreases as the skill levels up. Consume [Cage] x1"

4. Skillful Capture
Lv 1 Skill; No Limit
Passive Effect: Supposedly gives a chance to increase the maximum level of captured pets.  At level 10, raise max pet’s level by 5 - 10 level
Game Description: "Chance to raise the growth limit level of the captured monsters."
 
5. Careful Capture
Lv 3 Skill; No Limit
Passive Effect: Supposedly gives a chance to get captured pets with higher potential than usual. At level 10, raise pet’s potential stats by 1 - 10

6. Pet Heal
Lv 1 Skill; No Limit
MP Cost: 100
Base Charge Time: 6 seconds (levels 1 to 3); 5 seconds (level 4 to 6); 4 seconds (level 7 to 9); 3 seconds (level 10)
Skill Effect: Restores your pet's HP based on the following formula:
HP Heal Percentage = 0.02 (level 1); 0.05 (level 2); 0.07 (level 3); 0.1 (level 4); 0.12 (level 5); 0.15 (level 6); 0.17 (level 7); 0.2 (level 8); 0.22 (level 9); 0.25 (level 10) 
HP Heal = pet's Max HP * HP Heal Percentage + 100 * Skill Level 
Game Description: "Restore the called pet's HP. Unavailable on other players' pets."
Bow bonus: HP Heal Percentage +0.15
Bow bonus: Charge Time is halved
Staff bonus: HP Heal Percentage +0.15
Staff bonus: Charge Time is divided by 1.5
If this skill is used in a combo, it will not activate if you have no pet called
7. Pet MP Charge
Lv 2 Skill; No Limit
MP Cost: 300
Base Charge Time: 9 seconds (levels 1 to 3); 8 seconds (level 4 to 6); 7 seconds (level 7 to 9); 6 seconds (level 10)
Skill Effect: Restores (100 * Skill Level) MP to your pet
Game Description: "Restore the called pet's MP. Unavailable on other players' pets."
Bow bonus: Charge Time is halved
Staff bonus: Charge Time is divided by 1.5
If this skill is used in a combo, it will not activate if you have no pet called
