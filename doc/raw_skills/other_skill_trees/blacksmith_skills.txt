Create Equipment
Lv 1 Skill; No Limit
Skill Effect:
Allows you to create equipment without having to spend any spina; Equipments crafted this way has a very low chance of getting 2-slot — as of right now, the chances of getting a 1-slot or a 2-slot from this skill is currently unknown —, unlike NPC blacksmith, which need spend spina and can't produce a 2-slotted equipment.
Note: NPC blacksmith & this skill, both still have same materials requirements needed to craft!

The equipments created that way do not have stats, but have a unique stat called Potential; on success, the resulting Potential of an equipment depends on the equipment's base Potential, your stat allocations (this effect depends on the equipment type you're crafting), the number of slots of the resulting equip and certain skills that affect the resulting Potential (full effects unknown; all equips crafted through Blacksmiths or dropped have a Potential of 0); on failure, the equip may have a lower Potential than the base potential

On success, the equipment may have a higher base ATK/DEF than normal and higher potential than normal;

Technical Information(more detail in ⁠crafting)
The equipments you craft have a difficulty; you can adjust the success rate with your "Difficulty Limit":
Difficulty Limit = Blacksmith Proficiency Level + TEC/2 + total DEX/6

The formula of crafting success when this skill is at level 10 is supposedly:
Crafting Success Rate = 10 + Difficulty Limit - equipment's Difficulty + STR/10

Game Description: "Create equipment by yourself. [Create Equipment] is required to attach stats."

Customize Equipment
Lv 1 Skill; No Limit
Skill Effect:
Allows you to add and modify stats to equipments who have a Potential higher than 0
The number of stating slots you have start at 3
If a stating slot is already occupied by a stat, it cannot be changed, but the value can be modified

Technical Information(more detail in ⁠statting)
The chances for adding stats are calculated as follows:
Stating Success Rate = 230 * Potential After Stating/Potential Before Stating + 100 + Skill Stating Bonus Rate

If potential before stating is lower than base potential of the equipment on the final step of stating then,
potential before stating=base potential

Adding negative stats will add (5 + TEC/10)% of the Potential cost the the equip's Potential

Adding stats from the same category will add a penalty to the Potential removed or added

Passive Effect:
Skill Stating Bonus Rate +(Skill Level)
Game Description: "Consume potential and attach stats to the equipment created by [Create Equipment]."
