Process Material
Lv 1 Skill; No Limit
Skill Effect:
Allows you to process items in your inventory and convert them to material points without having to spend Spina
Items processed through this skill will give material points at random, but will give at least (80 + Skill Level)% of the normal amount they provide
Game Description: "Process materials by yourself. The success rate increases as the skill levels up. It is a common skill in Smith and Alchemy Skills."
This skill's level is shared with the skill with the same name in the Smith Skills tree
Item Synthesis
Lv 1 Skill; No Limit
Skill Effect:
Allows you to create potions, ores and gems without having to spend Spina

Technical Information
The items you create have a difficulty; you can adjust the success rate with your "Difficulty Limit":
Difficulty Limit = Synthesis Proficiency Level + TEC/2 + total DEX/6

Synthesis Success Rate = (Synthesis Proficiency + TEC/2 + DEX/6 + INT/10 - Item Difficulty + Mid-Class Synthesis Skill Level + High-Class Synthesis Skill Level + Expert's Synthesis Skill Level + Expert's Synthesis II Skill Level) * (0.5 + 0.05 * Item Synthesis Skill Level)

Game Description: "You can synthesize items by yourself. The success rate is lower than the synthesists but you don't need Spina."

Synthesize Equipment
Lv 2 Skill; No Limit
Skill Effect:
Allows you to synthesise equipment characteristics without having to spend Spina or Orbs

You can select two of the same type of equipments to select the characteristics you want to keep or get rid of for the resulting equipment; the characteristics you can select are: Appearance (the base model of the weapon/armor/additionnal), Dyes (each dye slot represents one characteristic) and Stats (this includes ATK/DEF of the equipment, Stability of the weapon, the stats attached to that equipment, the number of slots the equipment has, the Crystas attached to said slots, the level of refine and the armor's remodel; you cannot select each of these individually)

If left alone, the resulting equipment will have its characteristics selected at random between the two base equips; you can lock one base weapon's characteristic to ensure that the resulting equipment has it

You can lock 1 characteristic to start with; you gain extra locks by raising your Synthesis Proficiency Level (one at level 50, one at level 100, one at level 150 and the final one at level 200)

On failure, some of the locks might not take effect

Technical Information(more detail in ⁠synthesis)
The chances for success are calculated as follows:
Equip Synthesis Lock Penalty = number of locked characteristics²/2; this value cannot go under 1
Equip Synthesis Success Rate = (50 + Skill Equip Synthesis Boost + 2 * TEC)/Equip Synthesis Lock Penalty

Skill Equip Synthesis Boost starts at 0
Passive Effect: Skill Equip Synthesis Boost +(10 * Skill Level)

Game Description: "You can synthesize items by yourself. The success rate is lower than the synthesis but you don't need Spina."
