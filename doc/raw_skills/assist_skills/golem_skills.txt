Golem Call
T1; [Active Skill] Bowgun/Staff/Main Magic Device
MP Cost: 500 MP

Summons a golem upon cast; it will do Golem's Basic Attack for you and can grant you damage reduction from Golem's Shield.

Base Cast Time: 2 Seconds
Base Buff Duration: 10 seconds
Base Shield Range: 4m

Golem's Properties:
Golem uses your character's status: Attack Speed (+Motion Speed% status is not included), Critical Rate, Critical Damage, Pierce, Stability, Character's Movement Speed (walking speed).
Golem's movement speed is based off on the character's default Movement Speed (no run/skill).

Golem's Attack Properties:
Element: Weapon Dependant
These Buffs do not apply to the Golem's Attack: Twin Storm, Dual Shield, Knife Combat, Shukuchi, Sliding, Bouncing Blade, Storm Reaper, Magic Arrow from Hunter Skills, Asura Aura.
Golem's Attack is affected by short/long range damage; not affected by Long Range skill; not affected by whack/concentrate; uses normal proration; deals no proration; affected by Role Model regislet; not affected by Zero Stance regislet.
Ex Skill: Golem Selection

You may choose one of the three Golem types available:

Lancer-type: charges at the target & has high attack power.
Damage Type: Physical (Uses MATK)
Base Attack Multiplier: 2
Maximum Attack Range: 1m
Movement Speed Coefficient: 75%

Shielder-type: stays near the user and attacks any target within range.
Damage Type: Physical (Uses MATK)
Base Attack Multiplier: 1
Maximum Attack Range: 7m
Movement Speed Coefficient: 50%
Base Damage Reduction (Golem's Shield): 10%

Buster-type: has long attack range and deals magic damage.
Damage Type: Magic (Uses MATK)
Base Attack Multiplier: 1
Maximum Attack Range: 14m
Movement Speed Coefficient: 25%
Ex Skill: Magic Lamp Power Distribution

Magic Lamp Count: Skill Level

:battery: Surplus:
Buff Duration: + 10 * Surplus Point seconds
-The golem lasts longer with more power left.

:attackg: Attack:
Golem's Attack Multiplier: + 0.2 * Attack Point
Golem's Accuracy: + 20 * Attack Point

:shieldg: Shield:
Damage Reduction: + 2% * Shield Point

:speedg: Speed:
Golem's Movement Speed Coefficient: + 15% * Speed Point

*Attack, Shield, and Speed Magic Lamp allocations get extra effects from Attack Boost, Shield Boost, and Speed Boost Skill.
Game Description: Summons a golem to assist in battle. Your attacks won't restore MP. Instead, the golem's normal attacks will. From the EX skill, you can choose the golem type and adjust the magic lamp.

Attack Boost
T3; [Passive Skill] Bowgun/Staff/Main Magic Device

Golem's Attack Multiplier: + 0.02 * Skill Level * Attack Point
Golem's Accuracy: + 2 * Skill Level^2

Reactivating "Golem Call" while a golem is on the field grants a one-time buff that boosts the golem's attack power.
Lancer:
Buffed Golem Attack Multiplier: Golem Attack Multiplier * (2 + 0.2 * Attack Boost Skill Level)
Buster:
Buffed Golem Attack Multiplier: Golem Attack Multiplier * (1 + 0.2 * Attack Boost Skill Level)
Shielder:
Buffed Golem Attack Multiplier: Golem Attack Multiplier * (2 + 0.2 * Attack Boost Skill Level) + (0.4 * Shield Point)
This effect affects the multiplier of one Golem basic attack after reactivating Golem Call.
Game Description: Improves the magic lamp to increase attack efficiency. Increases the boost received per lamp power distributed to Attack. Always increases Accuracy, no matter the distribution.
Bowgun/Staff/Magic Device (main) Bonus: Reactivating "Golem Call" while a golem is on the field grants a one-time buff that boosts the golem's attack power.
