Safe Rest
Increase Natural HP Regen
Passive Skill
Effect: When you're in off-battle, Increase Natural HP regen at the end of calculation
Amount of flat Natural HP regen (NHPR) = +10 * Lvl
Amount of % Natural HP regen (NHPR) = +10 * Lvl %
NOTES:
-- Natural HP regen from equip & avatar are added as base NHPR value. Meanwhile NHPR from skill is applied after base NHPR is calculated.
Base NHPR = MaxHP/25 * (100% + Total eq NHPR%) + 10 + Total eq flat NHPR
Final NHPR = Base NHPR * (100% + Total NHPR% skill) + Total flat NHPR skill
